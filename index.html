Who I Am

A Versatile Professional with Purpose

I'm Muppidathi Shanmugavel — a digital marketer, trainer, and community volunteer based in Thirunelveli, Tamil Nadu. My career has spanned HR operations, quality control in manufacturing, veterinary pharmaceutical sales, and now digital marketing and educator roles.

I specialize in helping organizations grow online through SEO, social media marketing, Google Ads, and content strategy. Equally, I'm passionate about empowering the next generation — having trained hundreds of college students in employability, communication, and digital skills through TN SKILLS Naan Mudhalvan programs.

Beyond professional work, I serve as a Block Coordinator under My Bharat Kendra and a Disaster Management Trainer certified by the National Disaster Management Authority.
